# Stanford AI Index monitoring

Use the official [2026 AI Index report](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report), its chapter pages, downloadable chapters, and Public Data as the primary research source. Never use a short-video subtitle or a reposted report cover as the evidence.

## Scan policy

- Daily and closing brief: include only a new release, correction, dataset update, or an AI Index finding that materially changes the interpretation of a current AI-chain event.
- Weekly report: scan the official AI Index page every week. If there is no material update, record the check internally and do not force an item.
- Annual deep read: map the most decision-relevant evidence from Research and Development, Technical Performance, Economy, Policy and Governance, Science, and Medicine to chips, cloud/data centers, software, electricity, healthcare, labor, and regulation.

## Citation detail

For every number preserve chapter, chart/table identifier or page, data year, unit, geography, and the original data provider named by Stanford. Stanford HAI is `primary-research`; a chart's underlying provider must remain visible when supplied.

## Market-use boundary

AI Index supports structural and 2–4 week or longer industry reasoning. It cannot by itself support a 1–5 trading-day price forecast. Short-horizon claims must add current earnings, guidance, orders, valuation, complete-session price/volume, and at least one other independent source.

Create original structured charts from cited values. Do not screenshot or redraw Stanford's report cover or charts with an image model.
